# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — a place on every screen

- Explicit monitor choice in Settings and the menu bar, saved by a stable display UUID with a disconnected-display fallback.
- The attached backdrop reaches the physical top edge across Spaces and full-screen transitions; text keeps menu-bar and camera-notch clearance.
- Automatic, cancellable provider retries with per-origin cooldowns, Retry-After, positive jitter and increasing delays. Refresh preserves other cached songs and cannot bypass a cooldown.
- A quieter waiting state, smaller attached panel, redesigned settings sidebar, grouped appearance controls and live typography preview.

## 1.0.0 — source preview

Initial Songcrumb developer release: native Apple Silicon menu bar app, attached and floating lyrics panels, monospaced typography, configurable appearance, public Apple Events player adapters, automatic metadata-matched LRCLIB lookup, licensed backend adapter and experimental paired YouTube Music companion. Includes original branding, a local project page, verification fixtures and documented manual release checks.
