# Songcrumb

**Keep the words close.**

A little Mac app that leaves a few words from your music within reach. The voice is warm, direct and lightly playful. Talk about the music and the person’s day. Avoid exaggerated battery claims, universal lyrics promises or making a desktop utility sound like a lifestyle movement.

## Identity

Use **Songcrumb** in prose and the app name; **songcrumb** is the lowercase wordmark. The original icon is a bitten record, with a lyric-like label and three small crumbs. It should feel musical at app-icon size and a little unexpected up close.

Use `assets/app-icon.svg` as the editable master, `assets/mark.svg` for a monochrome mark, and `assets/app-icon.png` for the 1024px export. The native app includes the matching multi-resolution AppIcon.icns.

## Interface

The default lyric panel is centered and flush beneath the macOS menu bar, with square top corners and gently rounded bottom corners. Monospaced lyrics use a medium active line and regular, faded context. Keep controls quiet. Unlocking attached mode enables interaction without moving the panel; Floating mode restores free positioning.

The project page uses paper, plum and restrained terracotta, with expressive editorial headings. Its desktop illustration and preview words are original. It must be labelled as an illustration, rather than presented as a live service screenshot. Its playback controls operate that demo only.

## Short introduction

Your music has something to say. Songcrumb keeps a few lines in view while you do your thing: a native macOS menu bar app that automatically finds lyrics and lets them follow the song.
